Uses the ideal 555 equations with thresholds at ⅓ and ⅔ VCC, so timing doesn't depend on VCC. The output high level is VCC − 1.7 V, like the ngspice model. KiCad will read about 0.3% longer because of the discharge transistor's on-resistance. The first high pulse is longer because C102 starts at 0 V.
